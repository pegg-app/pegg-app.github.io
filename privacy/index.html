Pegg — Privacy Policy (native iOS)

Effective Date: 14 October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Pegg ("Pegg," "we," "us," or "our"). Pegg is a fashion resale marketplace that helps you track your wardrobe, understand what it's worth, and sell with confidence. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we collect, use, and share it.

This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the information we gather when you use the Pegg website, mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the "Service"). By accessing or using Pegg, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use the Service.

We encourage you to read this policy in full so you understand your rights and our obligations. If you have any questions after reading, you can always reach us at support@pegg.app.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information. When you create an account, we collect information such as your name, email address, profile photo, and shipping address. If you choose to sell items, we may also collect additional identity verification details as required by applicable regulations and our payment processor, Stripe.

Account Identifier. When you create an account we generate and store a unique account identifier for you, provided by Google Firebase Authentication. It is how your wardrobe, listings, orders and messages are associated with you.

Payment Information. Pegg uses Stripe to process all financial transactions. When you add a payment method or set up a seller payout account, your payment card details and bank account information are collected and handled directly by Stripe in accordance with their privacy policy and PCI-DSS standards. Pegg does not store your full card number or full bank account number on our servers. We do store the limited payout details Stripe returns to us — your bank's name and the last four digits of the account — so we can show you where your money is going.

Wardrobe and Listing Data. When you add items to your wardrobe or create listings, we collect the photographs you upload, the descriptions you provide, and any associated metadata such as brand, size, condition, and category. This information is used to power our marketplace features and AI-driven pricing tools.

Receipts You Forward. Pegg gives you a private forwarding address. When you forward an order confirmation to it, we receive that email (its sender, subject, date, text and up to three attachments) through our email provider, Mailgun, and store it with your account so we can add what you bought to your wardrobe.

Gmail, If You Connect It. You can choose to connect a Gmail account so Pegg can find your order confirmations for you. Pegg asks Google for read-only access (the gmail.readonly permission) and for nothing else in your Gmail. With that access, our server:

  • searches your mailbox only for messages whose subject contains "order", "confirmation", "receipt", "purchase", "invoice", "dispatch" or "shipping", from the last 12 months on the first scan and since your last scan after that;
  • reads the sender and message identifier of each match, and, for each match it has not seen before, the message's sender, subject, date, text and up to three attachments;
  • stores those messages with your account, and the Gmail address you connected.

Pegg never sends, deletes, labels or changes any email, and never reads messages that do not match the search above. The access Google grants is kept only on our server, encrypted, and is never stored in the app on your phone.

Purchase and Sales History. When you buy or sell an item we record the transaction: the item, the price, the platform fee, the shipping address used, and the dates on which the order was placed, marked as posted by the seller, and confirmed as delivered by the buyer. This history is retained for as long as your account is open, because it is what the marketplace, your payout balance and any dispute are resolved against.

Marketplace Activity. We record which listings are viewed, and by whom, so that sellers can see interest in their items and so we can identify trending listings. Where you are signed in, this is associated with your account.

Technical Information. When you interact with the Service we collect the information needed to deliver and secure it, including your IP address and the dates and times of your requests.

The mobile app does not read any advertising identifier (IDFA), any vendor identifier (IDFV), or your location. It contains no advertising, attribution or analytics SDK. It does not record your marketplace searches.

Cookies (website only). Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and to understand how the site is used. The mobile app does not use cookies or tracking pixels.

3. How We Use Your Information

Providing and Improving the Service. We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and enhance Pegg. This includes facilitating transactions between buyers and sellers, managing your account, displaying your wardrobe and listings, processing payments through Stripe, and providing customer support. We continuously analyze usage patterns to improve the user experience and develop new features.

AI-Powered Pricing and Recommendations. Pegg uses artificial intelligence to analyze market trends, comparable sales data, and item characteristics to generate pricing estimates for your wardrobe items. The data you provide about your clothing — including photos, brand, condition, and category — is processed by our AI systems to deliver accurate valuations and personalized recommendations. We do not sell the outputs of this analysis to third parties.

Receipts and Gmail. We use the receipts you forward, and the messages found in a Gmail account you connect, for one purpose: to recognise the items you bought (name, brand, size, colour, price, date and retailer) and add them to your wardrobe for you to review. To do this, the text of each receipt is sent to an AI service (Anthropic, with OpenAI as a fallback) that extracts those details. We do not use your emails for advertising, we do not sell them, and we do not use them to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine learning models. People at Pegg do not read your emails, except with your explicit permission for a specific message (for example, to fix a receipt that did not import), when needed for security or to comply with the law.

Communications. We may use your email address and, if you have granted permission, push notifications, to send you transactional notices such as order confirmations, notice that one of your items has sold, reminders to post a sold item, and payout receipts, as well as service-related announcements. With your consent, we may also send promotional messages about new features, marketplace activity, or items we think you might like. You can opt out of promotional communications at any time, and you can turn off push notifications entirely in your device settings or per-category in the app.

Safety, Security, and Legal Compliance. We use your information to detect and prevent fraud, enforce our terms of service, verify identities where required, and comply with applicable laws and regulations. This includes monitoring transactions for suspicious activity and cooperating with law enforcement when legally obligated to do so.

Service Providers. We share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the Service:

Other Users and the Marketplace. Certain information is visible to other Pegg users as part of normal marketplace functionality. When you list an item for sale, your seller profile name, listing photos, descriptions, and pricing are displayed publicly. Buyers and sellers involved in a transaction may see each other's names and shipping information as needed to complete the sale — Pegg does not buy or provide shipping labels, so the seller posts the parcel themselves and needs the buyer's address to do it.

Legal Requirements. We may disclose your information if we believe in good faith that doing so is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, respond to a valid legal process (such as a subpoena, court order, or government request), protect the rights, property, or safety of Pegg, our users, or the public, or investigate potential violations of our terms of service.

Business Transfers. In the event that Pegg is involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganization, asset sale, or bankruptcy proceeding, your personal information may be transferred to the successor entity. We will notify you of any such change in ownership or control of your personal information by posting a notice on our website or sending you a direct communication.

5. Your Privacy Choices

Account Settings. You can review and update your personal information at any time in your account settings within the Pegg app. This includes your name, email address, profile photo, shipping address, and notification preferences. Changes take effect immediately upon saving.

Marketing Opt-Out. You may opt out of receiving promotional emails by clicking the "unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any marketing message or by adjusting your communication preferences in your account settings. Even if you opt out of promotional communications, we may still send you transactional and service-related messages that are necessary for the operation of your account.

Deleting Your Account. You can delete your account and your personal data from inside the app, without contacting us: Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is permanent. It removes your profile, your wardrobe items and their photographs, your listings, your saved items and follows, your notification preferences, the receipts you forwarded or that we found in Gmail, and your Gmail connection (we also ask Google to withdraw Pegg's access).

Disconnecting Gmail. You can disconnect Gmail at any time in Settings → Gmail → Disconnect. Pegg then asks Google to withdraw its access, deletes the encrypted access it held, and stops reading your mailbox. Items already added to your wardrobe stay until you delete them. You can also withdraw Pegg's access from your Google Account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Records of completed transactions are retained in a form that no longer identifies you, because a completed sale involves another person whose own purchase history and tax records we cannot lawfully erase. Where we are required by law or by our payment processor to retain financial records, we retain those records for the period required and no longer.

You may also request a copy of the personal information we hold about you by contacting support@pegg.app.

Cookies and Tracking (website only). Most web browsers allow you to manage cookie preferences through their settings. You can choose to block or delete cookies, although doing so may affect the functionality of certain parts of the website. We respect Do Not Track signals where technically feasible.

Google API Services: Limited Use

Pegg's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Pegg uses Gmail data only to add the items you bought to your wardrobe, a feature you can see and control in the app; does not transfer it to others except as needed to provide that feature (the AI services listed above), for security, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition; does not use it for advertising; and does not allow people to read it except as described in section 3.

7. Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect it against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encryption of data in transit using TLS, encryption of sensitive data at rest, access controls that limit data access to authorized personnel, and regular security audits of our infrastructure.

Payment information is handled by Stripe, which maintains PCI-DSS Level 1 certification — the highest level of security standard in the payments industry. Your card details never touch Pegg's servers directly.

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to promptly addressing any security incidents and notifying affected users as required by law.

8. Children's Privacy

Pegg is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that a child under 16 has provided us with personal information without verifiable parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information from our systems as promptly as possible.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to Pegg, please contact us at support@pegg.app so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
